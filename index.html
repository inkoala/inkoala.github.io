Pens, pencils, markers and watercolours, with the full colour picker and eraser.

Layers

Shape Pen

Draw a circle, square, triangle or line and hold at the end to make it clean.

Perspective guides

Turn on 1, 2 or 3-point guides and drag the focus points into place.

Ruler

Lay down straight lines whenever you need them.

Search and share

Name your drawings, find them fast, and share a file others can keep working on.

Build it up, layer by layer

Keep the sky, the mountains and the trees apart. Hide what you're not working on and move things around without starting again.

Get the angles right

Perspective guides sit over your canvas, so buildings and streets line up without the maths.
