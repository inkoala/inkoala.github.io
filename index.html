Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Inkoala does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • Your drawings stay on your device. They are saved only in the app's own folder on your iPad. There are no accounts, no servers and no cloud sync run by us.
  • No tracking or analytics. The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code.
  • Sharing is up to you. When you tap Export, the drawing file is handed to the iPad share sheet, and you choose where it goes (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files and so on). We never see it.
  • Importing. Files you import are copied into the app's folder on your device and nowhere else.
  • Children. Because no data is collected, none is collected from children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address.
