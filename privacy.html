Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Inkoala does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • Your drawings stay on your device. They are saved only in the app's own folder on your iPad or iPhone or iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers and no cloud sync run by us.
  • No tracking or analytics. The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code.
  • Sharing is up to you. When you tap Export, the drawing file is handed to the share sheet, and you choose where it goes (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files and so on). We never see it.
  • Live Sharing is off until you turn it on. When you share a drawing, the other devices connect straight to yours, over your Wi-Fi or directly between devices the way AirDrop does. Nothing goes through a server run by us or anyone else. Everything sent is end-to-end encrypted with a key that exists only in the QR code on your screen, so only people who scan that code can join or read anything. Others see the name and colour you choose in Settings, where you point, and the notes you choose to show.
  • Camera. The camera is only used to scan a sharing QR code. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Pictures you add are kept inside that drawing on your device or iPhone. Inkoala only sees the photos you pick, not your photo library.
  • Importing. Files you import are copied into the app's folder on your device and nowhere else.
  • Children. Because no data is collected, none is collected from children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address.
